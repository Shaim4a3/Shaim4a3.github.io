Ethics

  • Do not copy without giving credit.
  • Update and improve code and make it efficent.
  • Design software which is easy to understand for teams
  • Clear communication between teams
  • Take Responibility for actions
  • Regulary Audit logs
  • Avoid Biased Algorithms
  • Make sure to be transparent with all work
  • Be professional
  • Contribute to projects of others
  • Share your knowledge with your team
  • Admit what you did, be tranparent to your team
